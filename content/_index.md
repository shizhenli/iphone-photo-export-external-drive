symptoms:
- title: The copy stops partway
  text: The progress bar freezes, the iPhone disconnects, or the drive ejects before the export reports completion.
- title: Live Photos arrive as stills
  text: The still image is present but the paired motion clip is missing, so the photo no longer behaves like a Live Photo.
- title: Dates or folders look wrong
  text: Files land in an unexpected folder, dates change, or the destination count does not match the iPhone selection.
checks:
- title: Export a small, named sample
  text: Choose 20–30 items including at least one video and one Live Photo. Export to a new folder rather than merging into an existing library.
- title: Compare counts and types
  text: Count stills, videos, and Live Photo pairs on both sides. A Live Photo should produce the still and its paired motion asset, not just one file.
- title: Open files from the drive
  text: Disconnect the iPhone, reconnect the drive, and open several files from Finder or Explorer. Check dates, orientation, audio, and motion.
- title: Keep the source until verified
  text: Do not delete from iPhone or iCloud until the sample and a second random sample both pass. Record the export folder and any exceptions.

faqs:
- q: Will you delete photos from my iPhone?
  a: No. The source stays in place until you verify the destination and choose to remove it yourself.
- q: Why do Live Photos split into two files?
  a: A Live Photo is a still plus a paired motion asset. Some export methods copy only the still; we check for both.
- q: Can you recover an interrupted export?
  a: We can identify what is missing and repeat a controlled export. Recovery of files that were already deleted is outside scope.
