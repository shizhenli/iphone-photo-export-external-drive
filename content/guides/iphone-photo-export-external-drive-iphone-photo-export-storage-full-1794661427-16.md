You are copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halts with a storage full message before you can confirm which side is actually out of room.

## Check the destination drive first

Connect the drive to your computer and open its properties or Get Info. Compare free space to the size of the iPhone photo library. If free space is smaller, the export cannot finish. Also confirm the drive is formatted for writing (exFAT or APFS for Mac use; exFAT for cross-platform). A read-only format can surface as a write failure that looks like a space problem.

## Check the iPhone side

On the iPhone, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage. If the phone is nearly full, iOS may refuse to prepare or stage files for export. Look for a large Photos library and any pending iCloud sync. Freeing a small buffer, such as 2-5 GB, is often enough to let the transfer proceed. Do not delete originals yet.

## Interpret the exact result

If the destination shows less free space than the library size, the drive is the bottleneck. If the phone shows almost no free space, the phone is the bottleneck. If both have room but the export still stops, the drive may be disconnecting or using an incompatible format.

## Verification test

Create a small test folder on the drive and copy 20-30 photos, including one video. If that succeeds, the connection and format are sound, and the original failure was capacity-related. If it fails, treat it as a drive or cable issue before retrying the full export.
