You are copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halts with a storage full message before you can finish a controlled diagnosis.

## Check the Destination First

Connect the drive to your Mac or PC and open its properties or Get Info. Note free space and the total size of the iPhone photo library. If free space is less than the library size plus 10 percent, the export will stop. Also check for hidden folders such as .Trashes or .Spotlight-V100 on the drive; these can consume space without appearing in Finder. Empty the trash on the drive and retry.

## Check the iPhone Side

On the iPhone, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage. If the phone reports less than 5 GB free, iOS may refuse to prepare the export. Offload large apps or delete recently deleted photos, then restart the phone before retrying.

## Run a Verification Test

Create a small test folder on the drive and export just 10 photos. If that succeeds, the connection and format are fine and the issue is capacity. If it fails, note the exact message and stop; do not keep retrying, as repeated partial copies can leave orphaned files.

## Rollback and Recovery

If a partial export exists, do not delete it. Rename the folder to export_partial and keep it until the full copy verifies. If the drive is nearly full, move unrelated files off the drive rather than deleting the partial export. This preserves your recovery point if the next attempt fails.

If the **iphone photo export storage full** message persists after these checks, the drive may have a partition or format limit.
