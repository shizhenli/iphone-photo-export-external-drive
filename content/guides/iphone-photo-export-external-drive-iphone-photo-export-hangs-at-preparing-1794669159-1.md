You've connected your external drive, selected your photos, and started the export, but the progress bar stays on preparing and never begins copying. This guide walks you through a controlled diagnosis for when your **iphone photo export hangs at preparing**.

## Check the Drive's Free Space and Format

First, confirm the external drive has enough free space for the entire selection. On your iPhone, open Files, tap the drive, and check available storage. If free space is less than the total size of the photos, the export may stall at preparing. Also verify the drive format is compatible (exFAT or APFS for larger files); a FAT32 drive cannot hold files over 4GB and may cause the export to hang.

## Inspect the Photo Selection for Problem Files

A single corrupt or unsupported file can block the preparing phase. Try exporting a small batch of 10–20 photos. If that succeeds, the issue is within the larger selection. Narrow it down by exporting half at a time until you isolate the problematic file. Remove or convert that file, then retry.

## Restart the Photos App and iPhone

Force-close the Photos app by swiping up from the bottom and flicking the app away. Then restart your iPhone. Reconnect the drive and attempt the export again. This clears temporary glitches that can cause the preparing step to freeze.

## Verification Test

After these steps, start a new export with a small batch. If the preparing screen completes and copying begins within 30 seconds, the issue is resolved. If it still hangs, the drive or cable may be faulty.

## Rollback and Recovery

If the export fails partway, do not delete the original photos from your iPhone until you have verified the copies on the drive. Eject the drive safely, reconnect it, and check that the exported files open correctly. If files are missing or corrupted, delete the partial export and start over with a smaller batch.
