You start copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the transfer stalls before the whole library is copied.

## Check the drive's free space and format

Open Files on your iPhone, long-press the external drive, and choose Get Info. Confirm free space is larger than the photo library size. If free space is close to the library size, the export can stop near the end. Also confirm the format is exFAT or APFS; a read-only or NTFS drive will accept some writes then fail. If the drive is full or read-only, stop and free space or reformat before retrying.

## Check for a locked or sleeping connection

Unlock the iPhone and keep the screen awake during the copy. If the drive is connected through a hub or adapter, plug it directly into the iPhone. A power-saving disconnect can make an **iphone photo export interrupted** mid-transfer. After reconnecting, check whether the Files app shows a partial folder on the drive.

## Verification test

Create a small test folder on the drive and copy 20–30 photos into it. If that copy completes and the files open, the connection and format are sound. Then retry the full export, but copy in batches (for example, by year) rather than all at once.

## Rollback and recovery

Before deleting anything from the iPhone, open several copied photos on the drive to confirm they are intact. If the export stopped partway, delete only the incomplete folder on the drive, not the iPhone originals, then resume in smaller batches. Keep the iPhone library untouched until the verification copy is confirmed.
