You're copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the export starts and then stalls or stops before the whole library is copied. This is a common symptom of **iphone photos not exporting to external drive** when the transfer gets interrupted mid-process.

## Check the drive's free space and format

First, confirm the drive has enough free space for the entire library. On your Mac, open Finder, select the drive, and check the available space. If it's less than the total size of your photo library, the export will stall. Also verify the drive format is compatible (exFAT or APFS for Mac; exFAT for Windows). A read-only or NTFS-formatted drive will cause the export to stop early.

## Inspect the connection and power

A loose or underpowered connection can interrupt the transfer. Use a direct USB port, not a hub, and ensure the drive has its own power supply if required. Try a different cable or port. If the drive disconnects during the export, the process will halt.

## Verification test

Create a small test folder with 10–20 photos and export it to the drive. If that completes, the issue is likely scale-related (space or timeouts). If it also stalls, the problem is the drive or connection.

## Rollback and recovery

If the export stopped partway, don't delete anything yet. Eject the drive safely, reconnect it, and check which files were copied. You can resume by selecting only the remaining photos. If the drive shows errors, run First Aid (Disk Utility) before retrying.
