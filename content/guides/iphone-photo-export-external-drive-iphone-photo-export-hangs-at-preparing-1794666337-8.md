You start exporting your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the progress bar never moves past the preparing stage. This guide helps you diagnose why the **iphone photo export hangs at preparing** and get the copy started.

## Check the Drive's Free Space and Format

First, confirm the external drive has enough free space for the entire library. On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage to see the photo library size. Then connect the drive to a computer and check its available space. If free space is less than the library size, the export may hang at preparing. Also verify the drive is formatted as exFAT or APFS; FAT32 has a 4GB file size limit that can stall large exports.

## Inspect the Cable and Connection

A loose or faulty cable can interrupt the handshake during preparation. Disconnect and reconnect the drive firmly. Try a different cable or port if possible. If the drive has an LED, confirm it stays lit. A drive that powers off briefly during preparation will cause the export to hang.

## Restart the Photos App and iPhone

Force-close the Photos app by swiping up from the bottom and flicking the app card away. Then restart your iPhone. This clears temporary glitches in the export queue. After restarting, reconnect the drive and attempt the export again.

## Verification Test

Create a small test album with 5–10 photos and export only that album to the drive. If the test export completes, the issue is likely related to library size or a specific corrupted photo. If it still hangs at preparing, the problem is with the drive or connection.
