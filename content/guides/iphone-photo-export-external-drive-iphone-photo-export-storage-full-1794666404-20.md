You start copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halts with a storage full message before you can finish diagnosing it.

## Check destination free space first
Connect the drive to your computer and open its properties or Get Info. Compare free space to the total size of the photos you selected. If free space is less than the selection size plus 10 percent, the drive is the bottleneck. Delete unrelated files or move them elsewhere, then empty the trash so the space is actually reclaimed.

## Check iPhone free space
On the iPhone, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage. If available space is under 2 GB, iOS may refuse to stage export data. Offload large apps or delete recently deleted photos, then restart the phone. A restart clears temporary export caches that can hold space.

## Interpret the result
If both sides show enough free space but the export still stops, the failure is likely a stale temporary file rather than true capacity. This is the classic **iphone photo export storage full** trap: the message reports space, but the copy process cannot allocate it.

## Verification test
Create a new folder on the drive and copy only 20 photos to it. If that succeeds, the drive and cable are fine and the original selection was too large for one pass. Export in batches of 500 photos instead.
