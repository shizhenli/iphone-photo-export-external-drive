You start copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the export stalls before the whole library is copied. The progress bar freezes, and you are left with a partial copy and no clear error. Here is how to diagnose and recover without losing what has already transferred.

## Check the drive connection and free space

First, confirm the external drive is still mounted and has enough free space. On a Mac, open Finder and look for the drive under Locations. If it has disappeared, the export cannot complete. Also check the drive's available capacity: the full library must fit with some headroom. If free space is less than the library size, the export will stop partway. Result interpretation: if the drive is missing or nearly full, that is your stall cause.

## Inspect the current partial export

Open the destination folder on the external drive. Count the files or note the last successful item. If the export stopped at a consistent point, such as a specific date or album, the issue may be a problematic file or a Live Photo pair. If the folder is empty or only has a few items, the stall happened early, likely a connection or permission issue.

## Run a verification test

Disconnect and reconnect the drive, then export a small test batch of 20–30 photos to a new folder. If that completes, the original stall was likely a temporary glitch or a single bad file. If the test also stalls, the problem is with the drive or cable. Try a different cable or port.
