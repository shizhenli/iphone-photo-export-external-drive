You start copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the progress bar freezes and the export never finishes. This is a common case of an **iphone photo transfer stuck** at the final stage.

## Check the Drive and Connection

First, confirm the drive is still mounted and writable. Open Files or Finder and try creating a small test folder on the drive. If that fails, the drive dropped offline or is read-only. Reconnect it directly, not through a hub, and try again. Also check free space: the drive needs more than the library size, because the export writes temporary data before finalizing.

## Check the iPhone Side

On the iPhone, open Settings > Photos and confirm the device has enough free storage. A nearly full iPhone can pause an export mid-copy. Then lock the screen and keep the device connected. If the export stalls again, force-quit the Photos app and retry with a smaller batch: select one album or a range of dates rather than the entire library.

## Verification Test

After the retry, count the files in the destination folder and compare that number to the item count shown in Photos for the selected range. If the counts match and the progress bar completes without freezing, the export succeeded. If the count is short, the stall is repeatable and points to a specific problematic item or a failing cable.
