Your iPhone photo export to an external drive starts copying but then stalls or stops before the whole library is transferred, leaving you unsure whether to wait or restart.

## Check the drive and connection first

Confirm the external drive is formatted for the Mac or PC you are using (APFS or exFAT for Mac, exFAT or NTFS for Windows). A read-only format will let the copy begin and then fail partway. Next, check free space on the drive: the export needs room for the full library plus overhead. If free space is less than the library size, the transfer will stall near the end. Also connect the drive directly, not through a hub, and use the cable that came with it.

## Interpret the stall

If the progress bar freezes at the same point each time, note the photo count shown. A stall at a consistent count often means one file or a Live Photo pair is causing the block. If it stalls at a random point, suspect power or the cable. Keep the iPhone unlocked and the screen on during export; a locked phone can pause the transfer.

## Verification test

Create a small album of 20 photos, including one Live Photo, and export only that album to a new folder on the drive. If that completes, the drive and cable are fine and the issue is in the larger library. If it stalls, replace the cable and try a different port before retrying.

## Rollback and recovery

Do not delete anything from the iPhone until you have verified the copied files open on the drive. If an export is interrupted, delete the partial folder on the drive before retrying so duplicate or corrupt files do not accumulate.
