You started copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halted with a storage full message before you could confirm which side ran out of room.

## Check the destination drive first

Connect the drive and open its properties or Get Info. Compare free space to the reported size of the photo selection. If free space is smaller than the selection, the drive is the limit. If free space is clearly larger, move to the phone side.

## Check the phone's working space

On the iPhone, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage. A nearly full phone can stop an export because iOS needs temporary working room. If free space is under a few gigabytes, that is your likely cause. This is the classic **iphone photo export storage full** stop, and it happens before any controlled diagnosis.

## Look for leftover partial copies

On the drive, find the export folder and check for incomplete files or a temporary folder from the stopped run. Delete only those partial copies, not your originals. On the phone, remove recently deleted photos only after confirming they are safely on the drive.

## Verification test

Export a small batch, about 20 photos, to a new folder on the drive. If it completes and the files open, the space issue is resolved for that batch size. If it stops again, note whether the drive or phone reports the error; that tells you which side still needs space.

## Rollback note

If you deleted anything from the phone during diagnosis, restore it from Recently Deleted or iCloud before retrying. Keep the original library intact until a full export verifies.
