You're copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halts with a storage full message before you can finish.

## Check destination free space first

Connect the drive to your computer and check its available space. Compare it to the total size of the photos you're exporting. If free space is less than the export size, the drive is the bottleneck. Delete or move unrelated files to free at least 10% more than the export size. If the drive is nearly full, the export cannot complete.

## Check iPhone storage

**iPhone photo export storage full** errors can also come from the phone itself. Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage. If available space is under 1 GB, iOS may block the export. Offload large videos or delete recently deleted photos (Albums > Recently Deleted) to free space. Aim for at least 2 GB free before retrying.

## Verify the drive format and connection

Confirm the drive is formatted exFAT or APFS for large files. A FAT32 drive caps individual files at 4 GB, which can trigger a false storage full message on large videos. Also reseat the cable and try a direct port, not a hub.

## Run a verification test

Create a small test folder on the drive and export 10 photos. If that succeeds, export in batches of 500 rather than the entire library at once. If the test fails, note the exact error and free space again.

## Rollback and recovery

If the export stopped midway, don't delete the original photos from your iPhone. Remove any partial copies from the drive, free space, and restart the export from the beginning of the batch. Keep the iPhone library intact until you confirm the drive copies open correctly.
