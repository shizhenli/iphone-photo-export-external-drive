You start copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the progress bar freezes and the export never finishes. This is a common case of an **iphone photo transfer stuck** mid-process, and it usually points to a drive or file-system limit rather than a broken cable. Here is how to diagnose it step by step.

## Check the Drive Format and Free Space

Connect the drive to your Mac or PC and open its properties. Confirm the format is exFAT or APFS; NTFS is read-only on iPhone and will stall writes. Verify free space exceeds your photo library size by at least 10 percent. If either check fails, reformat the drive (after backing up its contents) or free space, then retry the export.

## Count the Files Already Copied

Open the destination folder and note how many files exist. Compare that number to your iPhone's photo count. If the export stops near a round number, such as 999 or 4,000, the drive may be hitting a folder-item limit. Create a new subfolder and export the remaining photos there.

## Run a Verification Test

Export a small album of 20 photos to a fresh folder on the same drive. If that completes, the drive and cable are fine and the issue is volume-related. If it stalls again, try a different cable or port before assuming the drive is failing.
