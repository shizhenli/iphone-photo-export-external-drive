You started copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the export stalled or stopped before finishing, and now some Live Photos appear missing.

## Check the export state before retrying
Open the external drive in Finder or File Explorer and sort by date modified. If the last copied files are recent and the drive has free space, the stall is likely a connection or power issue, not a full drive. If the drive is nearly full, stop and free space first. Also confirm the cable is connected directly, not through a hub.

## Identify whether Live Photos are truly missing
In the iPhone Photos app, open a photo you expected to export and check for the LIVE badge. On the drive, a Live Photo should appear as a still image plus a short .mov file with a matching name. If the .mov is absent, the export was interrupted before the paired video was written. This is the core of **iPhone Export Interrupted or Live Photos Missing: How to Reconcile**—the still and video are separate files, so a stall can split them.

## Resume safely
Do not delete the partial export. Create a new folder on the drive and export only the date range or albums that did not complete. Keep the iPhone awake and connected until the progress bar finishes. If it stalls again at the same point, note the last file copied; that file may be corrupt or too large for the drive format.

## Verification test
Export a small album of five Live Photos to a test folder. Confirm each still has a matching .mov file and that both open. If they do, repeat for the remaining ranges.
