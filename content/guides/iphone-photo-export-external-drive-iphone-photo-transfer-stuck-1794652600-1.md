You're copying your entire iPhone photo library to an external drive, but the export stalls or stops before finishing, leaving you unsure how to proceed.

## Check the Drive's Free Space and Format

First, confirm the external drive has enough free space for the full library. On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage to see the photo library size. Then connect the drive to a computer and check its available space. If free space is less than the library size, the export will stall. Also verify the drive format is exFAT or APFS; FAT32 cannot handle files over 4GB and may cause silent failures.

## Inspect the Connection and Power

A loose or underpowered connection can interrupt large transfers. Use a direct cable connection, not a hub, and ensure the drive has external power if required. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Photos and confirm "Transfer to Mac or PC" is set to "Keep Originals" to avoid format conversions that can stall. If you see the drive disconnect and reconnect in Files, the cable or port is the culprit.

## Run a Verification Test

Create a small test album with 10–20 photos and export it to the drive. If that completes, the issue is likely library size or a specific corrupt file. If it also stalls, the problem is the drive or connection. This test isolates the failure without risking the full library.

## Rollback and Recovery

If the export stopped midway, do not delete the partial copy yet. Eject the drive safely, reconnect, and try exporting a smaller batch. If the **iphone photo transfer stuck** issue persists, note the exact photo where it stops—that file may be corrupt. You can skip it by exporting in batches.
