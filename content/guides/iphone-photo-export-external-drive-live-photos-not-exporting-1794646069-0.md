You started copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export stalls or stops before the whole library is copied.

## Confirm where the stall happens

Watch the export progress for two minutes without touching the cable. If the counter freezes on the same item, note whether that item is a Live Photo (a still paired with a short video). Open Photos on the iPhone, find that item, and check that the LIVE badge appears. If the export stops at a Live Photo every time, the paired video component is the blocker, which is the usual cause of **live photos not exporting** during a library copy.

## Rule out the drive and cable first

Connect the drive directly to the iPhone without a hub. Check the drive's free space against the library size in Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Photos. If free space is less than the library size plus ten percent, the export can halt partway. Also confirm the drive format is readable by iOS; exFAT or APFS is safer than NTFS.

## Verification test

Create a small album containing five ordinary photos and one Live Photo. Export only that album to the drive. If the ordinary photos copy and the export stops at the Live Photo, the diagnosis is confirmed. If the whole album copies, the original stall was likely space or connection related.

## Recovery note

Do not delete anything from the iPhone until you have opened several copied files from the drive and confirmed they play. If an export fails midway, remove the partial folder on the drive before retrying so the next copy starts clean.
