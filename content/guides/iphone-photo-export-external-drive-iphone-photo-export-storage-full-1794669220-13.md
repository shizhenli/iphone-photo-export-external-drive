## Confirm which storage is actually full

You are copying your iPhone photo library to an external drive, and the export halts with a storage full message before you can tell whether the phone or the drive is the bottleneck. Start by separating the two. On the iPhone, open Settings > General > iPhone Storage and note the free value. On the computer or drive utility, read the destination volume's available space. Compare both against the size of the export batch, not the whole library. If the drive shows less free space than the batch size, the destination is the limiter; if the phone shows little headroom, iOS may be unable to stage files for transfer.

## Check for hidden staging files

A failed or repeated export can leave partial copies and temporary packages on the destination. Sort the drive by date and look for folders matching the export name with partial contents. These consume space without completing your library. Move them to a scratch folder rather than deleting immediately, so you can confirm they are duplicates.

## Run a controlled verification test

Create a small album of 20-30 photos, including one video, and export only that album to a new folder on the drive. If it completes, storage was the blocker and you can resume in batches sized to your free space. If it stops again, the issue is not capacity; note the exact message and stop repeating the full export.
